The scholarship

The Glenford Boothe Memorial Scholarship gives students in need at Clarendon College the support to stay in school and keep learning. It is our way of giving back to the community that raised him and the values he carried.

Through this fund, we honor Glenford’s name by helping students overcome financial barriers and keep moving forward with hope, purpose, and dignity.

Who it helps

Students at Clarendon College who face financial hardship.

Where gifts go

Directly to the scholarship fund for Clarendon College students.

Why it matters

An education opens doors, and no student should lose theirs over cost.
